Pricing

Sinfonie never bills for AI usage. Your Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok subscriptions and API keys stay yours and are paid to their vendors.

Free

$0

Parallel agents on your Mac, forever.

  • Unlimited single-repo workspaces
  • One space with up to two repositories
  • One account per vendor
  • Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok and API models

Pro

$15 / month, or $120 / year

For people who work across several repositories.

  • Unlimited spaces and repositories per space
  • Several accounts per vendor
  • Crew of models
  • Review cockpit, Jira, Linear, on-call agent

Team

$30 / seat / month, or $300 / seat / year

Shared spaces for the whole team.

  • Everything in Pro
  • Space definitions shared with your team
  • Admin, invites and central billing
  • Invoices for your company

Upgrade from Settings → Plan inside the app. Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record; prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT where it applies. See the refund policy and terms.

Questions

Do I need an account for the free plan? No. Signing in with GitHub is only needed to buy a plan and carry it between Macs.

What happens to my spaces if I stop paying? Nothing is deleted. Existing spaces keep working; you cannot add new ones beyond the free limit.

Can I use my own API keys instead of a subscription? Yes, on every plan. Sinfonie does not mark up tokens.
