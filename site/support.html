Support

We read everything. Most replies within two working days.

  • Inside the app: the feedback button in the sidebar (⇧⌘F) sends a message together with the app version and, if you choose, recent error logs.
  • On the web: the feedback form on the home page.
  • By email: hello@sinfonie.dev.

Slack connection

Signing in. Settings → Integrations → Slack → Sign in with Slack. Approve in the browser; the app connects on its own within a few seconds. If it does not, paste the code shown on the confirmation page into the field under the button.

Admin approval. Some workspaces require an admin to approve new apps. Slack shows a request screen in that case; once an admin approves Sinfonie, sign in again.

Disconnecting. Settings → Integrations → Slack → Disconnect removes the token from your Mac. You can also remove Sinfonie under your Slack workspace’s app settings.

What it posts. Nothing, unless you press Send on a specific reply in the On call view. Replies are posted as you.

Updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions and offers them in the sidebar. Downloads: sinfonie.dev/download.
